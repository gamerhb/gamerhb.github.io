How I work

Business context first. Technical depth when it matters.

I’m most useful when a problem crosses boundaries: operations + data, finance + modeling, product + testing, or customer research + execution.

Map the real workflow

Who does what, where information breaks, what decisions are delayed, and what constraints actually matter.

Build the smallest useful system

A model, workflow, prototype, requirement set, or decision framework that makes the next step clearer.

Validate the result

Use tests, reconciliation, source checks, sensitivity work, field feedback, or explicit acceptance criteria instead of relying on the first output. If the result looks suspicious, investigate before defending it.
